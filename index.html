About the Lake

Pragathi Lake — A Community Lifeline Under Threat

Pragathi Lake has long been a critical water body in its urban ecosystem — supporting local biodiversity, groundwater recharge, and serving as a community landmark. Rapid urbanization, inadequate waste management, and sewage inflow have pushed it to a tipping point.

🏙️
Urban Context
Located in a densely populated zone, the lake is surrounded by residential areas directly impacted by its degradation.
💧
Ecological Role
Acts as a natural water reservoir, supporting groundwater levels and local fauna including birds and aquatic life.
⚠️
Current Threat
Uncontrolled sewage discharge, solid waste dumping, and encroachment have accelerated degradation over the past decade.
👥
Community Bond
Thousands of residents live within 1km radius — their health, property values, and quality of life are directly tied to the lake's condition.

Public Health

The Human Cost

Water stagnation directly fuels mosquito breeding cycles, creating vectors for life-threatening diseases across the surrounding community.

🦟
Dengue Fever
Aedes mosquitoes breeding in stagnant water transmit dengue, causing hemorrhagic fever in severe cases.
🩺
Malaria
Anopheles mosquito larvae thrive in the polluted, oxygen-depleted water, sustaining malarial transmission chains.
🌡️
Chikungunya
Joint-debilitating viral infection spread by the same Aedes mosquitoes breeding at the lake margin.
💨
Methane Release
Anaerobic decomposition of organic waste produces methane and hydrogen sulfide, causing respiratory illness.
🌊
Water Contamination
Groundwater infiltration spreads pollutants to nearby wells and supply systems used by local residents.
🧴
Skin Irritation
Direct contact with the water causes dermatitis and fungal infections in children who access the lake area.

Engineering Solution

How the System Works

Three integrated subsystems work in a self-reinforcing loop to restore and sustain the lake ecosystem.

Sunlight → Solar Panels → Aerators → ↑ Dissolved O₂ → Cleaner Water → Mosquitofish Thrive → ↓ Mosquito Larvae → Public Health Improves
☀️
Solar Aeration
Photovoltaic panels power submersible aerators, increasing dissolved oxygen levels to support aerobic bacteria and reduce stagnation. Components include a floating solar array, air pumps, diffusers, and battery storage.
🤖
Debris Collector
An autonomous catamaran-style robot equipped with ultrasonic sensors and a collection basket navigates the lake surface. It removes plastics, leaves, and floating waste to prevent drainage blockage.
🐟
Mosquitofish
Gambusia Affinis (mosquitofish) are introduced as biological control agents. They consume mosquito larvae at the source, dramatically reducing breeding without chemical pesticides.

Future Vision

Scaling to Every Urban Lake

The modular, self-funding architecture of this system makes it replicable across any urban water body — creating a template for India's 2,00,000+ lakes.

🧠
AI Water Quality Prediction
Machine learning models to predict quality degradation 72 hours in advance and trigger preemptive maintenance.
📱
Citizen Reporting App
Mobile platform for residents to report violations, monitor progress, and participate in governance.
🌐
IoT Sensor Network
Comprehensive dissolved oxygen, turbidity, and temperature monitoring grid across the entire lake basin.
🏛️
Government Partnership
Structured MOU framework for municipal corporations to adopt and fund scale-up to their water body portfolios.
🏙️
Multi-Lake Expansion
Standardized deployment kit enabling replication to other urban lakes within a 90-day onboarding timeline.
📊
Environmental Analytics
Automated ESG reporting dashboard for urban planners and policymakers to track restoration outcomes.
